    • About Us

    The New Paltz Club Soccer Team is a student-run organization that provides an opportunity for students to play competitive soccer while representing SUNY New Paltz. Our team is dedicated to fostering a sense of community, sportsmanship, and personal growth through the sport of soccer.

    We welcome players of all skill levels and backgrounds, and we strive to create an inclusive environment where everyone can enjoy the game. Our team participates in local leagues and tournaments, and we are committed to promoting the sport of soccer within our campus and the surrounding community.

    Join us on the field and be part of our journey as we continue to grow and achieve success both on and off the pitch!
